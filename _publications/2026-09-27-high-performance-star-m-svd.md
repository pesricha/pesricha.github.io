Tensor decompositions can compress large datasets better than traditional matrix methods while losing very little accuracy. In the star-M tensor framework, a tensor is decomposed in a matrix-mimetic way, which yields the star-M SVD. This SVD has optimality guarantees, but its software implementations have mostly been limited to productivity-oriented languages such as MATLAB and Python.

This paper presents a shared-memory parallel, high-performance implementation of the star-M SVD, aimed at efficiently compressing large scientific datasets.
